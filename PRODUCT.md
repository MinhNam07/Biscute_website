# BISCUTE — Product Definition

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web.

BISCUTE is a bilingual digital storefront and product catalogue for a physical souvenir shop in Hanoi.

The website does not process online orders.

---

## Product Type

**Digital storefront + product discovery catalogue.**

The website exists to help visitors:

1. Understand what BISCUTE is.
2. Discover products they may want to bring home.
3. Understand what makes BISCUTE different.
4. Find the physical store.
5. Open directions and visit BISCUTE Vietnam.

---

## Primary Audience

International tourists who are planning a trip to Hanoi or are currently visiting Hanoi, especially Hanoi Old Quarter.

They are looking for souvenirs that:

* feel distinctly connected to Vietnam;
* have a playful contemporary design;
* are easy to carry while travelling;
* work well as gifts;
* feel worth making a specific store visit for.

---

## Secondary Audience

Vietnamese visitors and domestic tourists interested in cute, contemporary Vietnam-inspired souvenirs and gifts.

---

## Job to Be Done

Find cute, distinctively designed Vietnam-inspired souvenirs that are easy to pack, feel worth bringing home, and are worth visiting a specific shop in Hanoi for.

---

## Product Purpose

BISCUTE Vietnam uses digital product discovery to drive physical store visits.

Visitors should be able to browse products, understand BISCUTE's design language, explore verified collections, check product information, and find the physical store before visiting.

The website is not an ecommerce store.

---

## Primary Conversion

**Open directions → visit BISCUTE Vietnam.**

Every major customer journey should ultimately make visiting the physical store an obvious next step.

---

## Secondary Conversions

* Explore products.
* Open a product detail page.
* Understand the BISCUTE brand.
* Explore verified collections.
* Search for a specific type of product.
* View store information.

---

## Not a Goal

The website does not provide:

* online checkout;
* shopping cart;
* online payment;
* shipping;
* order management;
* customer accounts;
* realtime inventory management;
* marketplace functionality.

Do not introduce ecommerce patterns unless the product strategy explicitly changes.

---

## Brand Positioning

BISCUTE is a playful contemporary Vietnamese souvenir brand.

It should not feel like a generic souvenir stand, luxury gift shop, traditional handicraft website, or generic Hanoi tourism website.

BISCUTE differentiates through the combination of:

### 1. Distinctive BISCUTE Visual Language

Vietnam-inspired subjects are interpreted through BISCUTE's playful characters, artwork and graphic language.

The brand identity should come from BISCUTE itself, not generic Vietnamese decoration.

### 2. Travel-Friendly Products

Products are selected and presented with tourists in mind.

Packability, portability and gifting usefulness are meaningful product benefits.

### 3. Vietnamese Culture Reimagined

Vietnamese places, objects, food, everyday culture and other cultural references can become inspiration for BISCUTE products when supported by real product data.

The website should present Vietnamese culture through BISCUTE's visual perspective rather than reproduce generic tourism clichés.

---

## Brand Idea

**Vietnamese culture, reimagined the BISCUTE way.**

Supporting direction:

**Cute memories from Vietnam.**

The tagline may be used as brand copy, but it should not replace the stronger product positioning above.

---

## Voice

BISCUTE should feel:

* cute;
* playful;
* simple;
* cheerful;
* contemporary;
* approachable;
* tourist-friendly.

BISCUTE should not feel:

* childish;
* luxury;
* corporate;
* overly sentimental;
* traditionally ornamental;
* like a generic tourism campaign.

Copy should remain short, concrete and easy for international visitors to understand.

---

## Experience Principles

### 1. Store First, Not Checkout First

Online discovery serves physical store conversion.

Product pages and major browsing journeys should naturally lead toward visiting BISCUTE Vietnam.

---

### 2. Show the Product Early

Visitors should understand what BISCUTE sells within the first viewport or immediately after it.

Product imagery and BISCUTE artwork should have higher visual priority than generic Hanoi photography.

---

### 3. Packability Is a Product Benefit

When supported by product data, highlight useful travel attributes such as:

* easy to pack;
* lightweight;
* gift-ready.

Do not invent attributes for individual products.

---

### 4. BISCUTE Before Vietnam Tourism

The website should make visitors remember BISCUTE.

Vietnamese cultural context supports the brand but should not overpower it.

Avoid defaulting to generic motifs such as landmarks, flags, traditional patterns, lotus imagery or other tourism shorthand unless they are genuinely part of a BISCUTE product or artwork.

---

### 5. Bilingual by Design

English and Vietnamese must both be complete product experiences.

Because international tourists are the primary audience, English copy should be written as a primary communication language rather than treated as a literal translation of Vietnamese.

Vietnamese must maintain full content and functional parity.

---

### 6. Show, Don't Sell Online

Use product discovery language rather than ecommerce pressure.

Prefer:

* Explore products
* View product
* Find at BISCUTE
* Visit BISCUTE
* Open directions

Avoid:

* Buy now
* Add to cart
* Checkout
* Selling fast
* Hurry
* Low stock

unless the product model changes and corresponding data exists.

---

## Core Experience Scope

### Homepage

The homepage should help visitors:

**Discover BISCUTE
→ understand the style
→ see products
→ understand what makes BISCUTE different
→ explore verified collections when available
→ visit the physical store.**

The exact homepage section structure is a design decision and should not be treated as permanent product scope.

---

### Product Catalogue

Visitors can browse the available product catalogue.

The catalogue is for discovery, not ecommerce.

Product cards should prioritize:

* imagery;
* product name;
* relevant product classification;
* optional verified price information.

---

### Product Detail

Product pages may communicate:

* product images;
* product name;
* description;
* price;
* variants;
* specifications;
* inspiration or artwork story when verified;
* travel-friendly attributes when verified;
* physical-store availability wording appropriate to the available data.

The primary action should lead toward the physical store rather than checkout.

---

### Collections

Collections are thematic groups of real BISCUTE products.

**Exact collection taxonomy must come from verified product data.**

Do not invent collection names to complete a layout.

---

### Search

Visitors may search the catalogue to discover products.

Search remains a discovery tool rather than a commerce function.

---

### Visit

The Visit experience is a primary conversion surface.

It should clearly communicate:

* BISCUTE Vietnam;
* verified address;
* verified opening hours;
* map;
* directions;
* real store photography when available;
* verified contact information when available.

## Content Integrity

**Unknown means unknown.**

Unknown data should use `null`, an empty collection, or an internal `TBD` state.

Unknown information must not be invented to complete a page.

Do not fabricate:

* collections;
* bestseller status;
* scarcity;
* awards;
* reviews;
* customer testimonials;
* social accounts;
* contact information;
* store locations;
* product origin;
* artwork ownership;
* inventory status;
* historical brand claims.

---

## Marketing Claim Rules

Claims such as the following require evidence before publication:

* Best Seller
* Most Popular
* Limited
* Exclusive
* Original Artwork
* Handmade
* Premium Quality
* Sustainable
* Eco-Friendly
* Available Now
* Made in Vietnam

When evidence is unavailable, omit the claim rather than replacing it with speculative marketing copy.

---

## Product Data Principles

Product-specific claims must come from product data.

Examples include:

* price;
* dimensions;
* materials;
* variants;
* packability;
* weight;
* gift suitability;
* manufacturing origin;
* collection;
* release status.

A global brand statement must not automatically become an attribute of every SKU.

---

## Language Strategy

**Primary communication audience:** international visitors.

**Languages:** English and Vietnamese.

English should be simple, natural and tourist-friendly.

Vietnamese should maintain complete functional and content parity.

Locale routing and default-locale implementation may be determined separately from the product strategy.

---

## Accessibility Principle

The website should remain usable across keyboard, touch, screen-reader and reduced-motion contexts.

Detailed accessibility implementation belongs in the design and engineering specifications.

A formal WCAG conformance target should be defined before production launch.

---

## Decision Rule

Every meaningful page, section or interaction should support at least one of these outcomes:

1. Help visitors understand BISCUTE.
2. Help visitors discover products.
3. Help visitors understand why BISCUTE is different.
4. Help visitors visit the physical store.

If a component supports none of these outcomes, reconsider whether it belongs in the product.

---

## Product Success

The ideal customer journey is:

**Discover BISCUTE
→ understand the brand
→ find an interesting product
→ decide BISCUTE is worth visiting
→ open directions
→ visit the store
→ purchase in person.**

Website success should therefore be evaluated primarily through product discovery and store-intent behavior rather than ecommerce metrics.
